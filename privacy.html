Asaki 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 14 日

Asaki 是一个在你的 Mac 上运行的 App。它没有账户，也没有自己的服务器。我们（Asaki 的开发者）不收集你的任何数据，也看不到你在 Asaki 里写下的任何内容。

我们不收集什么

  • 不收集你的任务、日记、回顾、附件或设置。
  • 不收集使用统计、崩溃报告、设备信息或任何标识符。
  • 不跟踪你，不使用广告或分析服务。

你的数据存在哪里

这台 Mac。任务、日记、回顾和附件保存在 Asaki 自己的沙盒里，其他 App 无法读取。模型服务的密钥保存在这台 Mac 的钥匙串里，不会同步。

你的 iCloud（可选）。打开 iCloud 同步后，同样的内容也会保存在你自己 iCloud 账户的私有数据库里，用于在你的设备之间同步。这部分数据由 Apple 按照 iCloud 的条款保管，只有你的 Apple 账户能访问，开发者看不到。单个超过 10MB 的附件只留在本机，不会同步。iCloud 同步默认关闭，可以随时在设置里关掉。

什么时候会有内容离开这台 Mac

只有下面两种情况：

  1. iCloud 同步：打开同步后，见上文。
  2. 生成回顾：只有在你点击“生成回顾”时，所选时间范围内的待办和日记文字才会发送给你在设置里选择的模型服务，例如 DeepSeek、Kimi、通义千问，或者你自己填写的地址。如果你选择的是本机工具（Kimi Code 或 Qwen Code），内容由该工具使用你在其中登录的账号，发送给它对应的服务：Moonshot AI，或 Qwen Code 所连的模型服务。
    • 图片和附件不会发送。
    • 发送前，你可以查看将要发送的全文。
    • 每个服务第一次使用前，Asaki 都会先征得你的同意。
    • 生成的结果先给你预览，你确认后才会保存。
    • 收到内容的服务如何保存和使用它，由该服务自己的条款决定，与 Asaki 无关。

除此之外，Asaki 不会把任何内容发送到网络上。

购买

购买 Asaki Pro 由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。Asaki 只向 App Store 询问这个 Apple 账户是否买过 Pro，拿不到你的付款信息。

收录与剪贴板

Asaki 只在你主动收录时读取内容：按下“收录剪贴板”的快捷键时读取剪贴板，使用系统服务“收录到 Asaki”时读取你选中的文字或文件。读取到的内容只保存在你的 Asaki 里。Asaki 不会在后台读取剪贴板，也不会读取其他 App 的内容。

你可以随时

  • 在“记录”里把内容导出成普通的 Markdown 文件。
  • 删除任意一条记录、回顾或任务。
  • 关闭 iCloud 同步，或在设置里选择不使用任何模型服务。
  • 删除 Asaki 及其数据。如果打开过 iCloud 同步，可以在系统设置里 iCloud 的存储管理中删除 Asaki 在 iCloud 中的数据。

儿童

Asaki 不面向儿童，也不收集任何人的个人信息。

本政策的变更

如果 Asaki 处理数据的方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策，并修改上方的更新日期。

Asaki Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Asaki is an app that runs on your Mac. It has no accounts and no servers of its own. We, Asaki's developer, collect none of your data and cannot see anything you write in Asaki.

What we do not collect

  • Your tasks, journal, reviews, attachments or settings.
  • Usage statistics, crash reports, device information or identifiers of any kind.
  • We do not track you, and use no advertising or analytics services.

Where your things are kept

On this Mac. Tasks, journal entries, reviews and attachments are kept in Asaki's own sandbox, which other apps cannot read. Keys for model services are kept in this Mac's keychain and are never synced.

In your iCloud, if you choose. With iCloud sync on, the same things are also kept in the private database of your own iCloud account, to sync between your devices. Apple keeps them under iCloud's terms; only your Apple Account can reach them, and the developer cannot see them. Attachments larger than 10 MB stay on the Mac they were added on. iCloud sync is off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

When anything leaves this Mac

Only in these two cases:

  1. iCloud sync, when it is on, as above.
  2. Writing a review: only when you click “Write a Review” is the text of the tasks and journal entries in the chosen period sent, to the model service you chose in Settings, such as DeepSeek, Kimi or Qwen, or an address you entered. If you chose a tool on this Mac (Kimi Code or Qwen Code), the tool sends it, with the account you are signed in to there, to its own service: Moonshot AI, or the model service Qwen Code is set up with.
    • Images and attachments are never sent.
    • Before anything is sent, you can read all of it.
    • Asaki asks for your consent before using each service for the first time.
    • The review is shown to you first, and saved only when you say so.
    • How a service keeps and uses what it receives is up to that service's own terms.

Apart from these, Asaki sends nothing over the network.

Purchases

Buying Asaki Pro is handled by Apple through the App Store. Asaki only asks the App Store whether this Apple Account has bought Pro, and never sees your payment details.

Capturing and the clipboard

Asaki reads only what you ask it to keep: the clipboard when you press the “Save Clipboard” shortcut, and the text or files you selected when you use the “Save to Asaki” service. What it reads is kept only in your Asaki. Asaki never reads the clipboard in the background, and never reads other apps' contents.

At any time, you can

  • Export your things as plain Markdown files from Records.
  • Delete any entry, review or task.
  • Turn off iCloud sync, or choose no model service in Settings.
  • Delete Asaki and its data. If you used iCloud sync, Asaki's data in iCloud can be deleted from iCloud storage management in System Settings.

Children

Asaki is not directed at children, and collects no one's personal information.

Changes to this policy

If the way Asaki handles data changes, we will update this policy and the date above.
