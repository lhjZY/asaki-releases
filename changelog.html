Asaki 现在可以自己更新

  • 有新版本时会提示，同意后在后台下载，退出时安装
  • 设置的“关于”里可以开关“自动检查更新”，也可以立即检查
  • 应用菜单里新增“检查更新…”

Asaki now updates itself

  • When there is a new version, it says so, downloads it in the background once you agree, and installs it when you quit
  • Turn “Check for Updates Automatically” on or off, or check now, in Settings under About
  • New in the app menu: “Check for Updates…”
